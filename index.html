Our services

  • Business systems analysis, process modelling and service redesign

    We use industry-recognised standards such as BPMN to model, analyse and redesign business processes to make them more effective.

  • Programme and project management

    Our PRINCE2 qualified team have extensive experience of planning and delivering successful programmes and projects of any scale.

  • Relationship management

    We work closely with your organisation to build up your networks, strengthen relations between you and your key stakeholders, and develop strong communities.

  • Clinical risk management and safety engineering

    We work with your Clinical Safety Officer to develop processes and procedures that keep the design, development, implementation and use of your clinical systems safe, meeting the DCB0129 and DCB0160 safety standards.

  • Business case and benefits management

    We provide training, assurance and advice, using existing templates and research to make your business case as thorough and accurate as possible, with benefits management at the core of your processes from the start.

  • Network design and implementation

    We support you to design, implement and maintain your network infrastructure.
